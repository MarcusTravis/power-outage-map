For the peak period, UNE estimated availability of 1,020 MW and a maximum demand of 2,800 MW, a deficit of 1,780 MW, and, if those conditions held, a forecast affectation of 1,810 MW. The bulletin also said solar photovoltaic parks produced 2,115 MWh, with a maximum of 329 MW delivered in the midday period from that source, and that thermal-generation limitations were 479 MW. CiberCuba, in its Nov 25 daily summary, also reported those official national figures, and, citing Empresa Eléctrica de La Habana, said that at 3:30 a.m. Cuba time Wednesday, Nov 25 (2:30 a.m. CT), the Havana utility reported 51 distribution circuits affected and 163 MW of affectation in Havana, described as local reports rather than a national balance, and later recorded Wednesday morning restorations in several Havana circuits as well as some remaining local affectations attributed to the generation deficit. CiberCuba, in the same Nov 25 daily summary, also cited Empresa Eléctrica de La Habana as reporting at 3:33 p.m. Cuba time Wednesday, Nov 25 (2:33 p.m. CT), 117 circuits affected and 266 MW of affectation in the province, linked to the generation-capacity deficit, and said the utility also communicated restorations in several capital circuits, including areas of Arroyo Naranjo, La Lisa, Cerro, and Habana del Este.
