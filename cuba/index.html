CubaNational electric system (SEN)Generation capacity deficit (multiple thermal units offline or in maintenance), plus a Friday transmission-line trip that briefly isolated Granma1,930 MW forecast to be affected at peak on Nov 13 (1,900 MW deficit), according to UNE's morning bulletin. At 6:00 a.m. Cuba time, 900 MW was available, demand was 2,142 MW, and 1,245 MW was already affected; UNE estimated 1,600 MW affected at midday. Thursday's maximum affectation was 1,920 MW at 7:00 p.m. The morning forecast listed several thermal units offline or in maintenance, including Guiteras, Felton, Santa Cruz, Renté, and Nuevitas. CubaNet, citing a later UNE note, said unit 2 of the Ernesto Guevara plant in Santa Cruz went offline at 8:55 a.m. Cuba time due to an oil leak in a turbine bearing, leaving all three units at that plant out of service; that failure was not included in the morning peak forecast. On Friday, a fault on the 110 kV line to Santiago de Cuba fully disconnected Granma province from the national grid; the Electric Company of Granma said the province was reconnected at 1:27 p.m. Cuba time and had 20 MW in service at 1:50 p.m., with restoration continuing gradually. Las Tunas was isolated at midday by the National Load Dispatch and resynchronized at 1:24 p.m. with 15 MW, according to the provincial utility as reported by El Alambique and CiberCuba. No later official UNE figure for the actual evening peak was found as of Friday evening CT.——El AlambiqueNov 13, 2026, 6:03 p.m. (El Alambique timestamp; UNE morning bulletin and Friday afternoon provincial utility notices)
