At 6:00 a.m. Sunday, the grid had 925 MW available versus 2,265 MW of demand, leaving 1,340 MW unserved, and UNE estimated 1,500 MW affected at midday. UNE said Renté unit 3 was expected to add 45 MW at the Sunday evening peak. On Saturday, service was affected by a generation-capacity deficit for all 24 hours and remained affected through the early morning; the maximum Saturday affectation was 1,878 MW at 7:00 p.m. Saturday solar photovoltaic output was 3,116 MWh, with a maximum of 485 MW at midday. No official Sunday midday or evening-peak actual was found, and no official Monday UNE national bulletin had been published as of this recheck. CiberCuba, in a Nov 16 compilation updated at 6:15 a.m. Cuba time Monday, said it was still showing Sunday's figures because Monday's bulletin did not yet contain sufficient numbers, and that the national grid was not recorded as in a total collapse.
