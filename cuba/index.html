CubaNational electric system (SEN)Generation capacity deficit, with several thermoelectric units offline due to breakdowns and maintenance. UNE's Tuesday bulletin, published by Cubadebate, said that unit 8 of CTE Mariel, unit 1 of CTE Santa Cruz, and unit 2 of CTE Felton were in breakdown, and that unit 3 of CTE Santa Cruz, unit 5 of CTE Nuevitas, and unit 5 of CTE Renté were in maintenance; thermal generation was also limited by 426 MWUNE's Tuesday bulletin, published by Cubadebate, is the latest official national forecast found: 1,730 MW affected at the evening peak, with 1,050 MW available versus 2,750 MW demand (1,700 MW deficit), if the forecast conditions hold. At 6:00 a.m. Tuesday, the grid had 1,050 MW available versus 2,175 MW of demand, leaving 1,130 MW unserved, and UNE estimated 1,200 MW affected at midday. On Monday, service was affected by a generation-capacity deficit for all 24 hours and remained affected through the early morning; the maximum Monday affectation was 1,849 MW at 7:00 p.m. Monday. Monday solar photovoltaic output was 4,284 MWh, with a maximum of 666 MW at midday. The Tuesday bulletin no longer listed the Antonio Guiteras unit or unit 1 of CTE Felton among units in breakdown or maintenance; CiberCuba, in a Monday report, said the Antonio Guiteras plant synchronized with the National Electric System on Monday after five days of emergency repairs, after going offline on Wednesday, Nov 11. CiberCuba, citing the Empresa Eléctrica de La Habana, said that at 6:01 a.m. Tuesday La Habana reported 36 distribution circuits affected by a deficit, with 89 MW affected, and that at 3:00 a.m. Tuesday it had reported 44 circuits and 110 MW affected; the utility also reported overnight restorations on circuits C16, H443, OP319, and GC14. CiberCuba's Nov 17 daily summary, still citing the Empresa Eléctrica de La Habana, later said the utility reported afternoon affectations by a generation deficit in numerous circuits of the capital, including sectors of La Lisa, Santos Suárez, San Miguel del Padrón, Guanabacoa, and Luyanó, and local restorations on circuits GC12, MB920, SF583, and Pz19. It also said circuit C14 was restored at 2:04 p.m. Cuba time Tuesday (1:04 p.m. CT) and was reported affected again from 5:54 p.m. Cuba time Tuesday (4:54 p.m. CT). No official Tuesday evening-peak actual from UNE was found. On Monday evening, CiberCuba, citing the Empresa Eléctrica de Las Tunas, said the province was disconnected from the National Electric System at 6:02 p.m. Monday because of a system emergency; it did not give a customer count or a reconnection time, and no later official reconnection notice was found.
