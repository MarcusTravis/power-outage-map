CubaNational electric system (SEN)Generation capacity deficit, with several thermoelectric units offline due to breakdowns (Santa Cruz unit 1, Antonio Guiteras, Felton unit 2, and Renté unit 3) and maintenance (Santa Cruz unit 3, Nuevitas unit 5, Felton unit 1, and Renté unit 5); thermal generation is also limited by 251 MWUNE's Sunday bulletin, published by Cubadebate and attributed to UNE-MINEM, forecast 1,860 MW affected at the evening peak: 970 MW available versus 2,800 MW demand (1,830 MW deficit), if the forecast conditions hold. At 6:00 a.m. Sunday, the grid had 925 MW available versus 2,265 MW of demand, leaving 1,340 MW unserved, and UNE estimated 1,500 MW affected at midday. UNE said Renté unit 3 was expected to add 45 MW at the Sunday evening peak. On Saturday, service was affected by a generation-capacity deficit for all 24 hours and remained affected through the early morning; the maximum Saturday affectation was 1,878 MW at 7:00 p.m. Saturday solar photovoltaic output was 3,116 MWh, with a maximum of 485 MW at midday. No official Sunday midday or evening-peak actual was found. On Sunday, Empresa Eléctrica de La Habana reported generation-deficit outages of 236 MW on 101 distribution circuits at 6:00 a.m. Cuba time, and 220 MW on 105 circuits at 9:15 a.m. Cuba time, according to compilations by CiberCuba and El Alambique. CiberCuba's Nov 15 compilation also listed additional Empresa Eléctrica de La Habana generation-deficit circuit interruptions starting at 5:48 p.m. Cuba time Sunday, including circuits D1300, D1305, A1071, and GC7.
