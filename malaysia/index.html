The company said the incident was detected at 6:04 a.m. Thursday, Nov 26, Malaysia time, and that technical teams were carrying out a comprehensive assessment at the site to identify the cause and determine the fastest, most effective, and safest restoration method. The Star, in a Thursday report, said Sabah Electricity had reported an incident on its electricity transmission system at 6:04 a.m. that caused the outage, and that photographs circulating on social media showed a collapsed transmission tower and power lines obstructing the main Ranau-Sandakan road. Malay Mail, citing Sabah Electricity, likewise said an incident on the transmission system at 6:04 a.m. caused a power outage affecting the East CoastSabah Electricity, as reported by Sinar Harian at 1:19 p.m. Thursday,Sabah Electricity, as reported by Sinar Harian at 1:19 p.m. Thursday, Nov 26, Malaysia time (11:19 p.m. CT Wednesday, Nov 25), and by The Star at 12:40 p.m. Thursday (10:40 p.m. CT Wednesday), said eight east-coast districts were affected: Sandakan, Tawau, Lahad Datu, Semporna, Kunak, Beluran, Telupid, and Kinabatangan. The company said technical teams were carrying out a comprehensive assessment at the site, that mobile generator sets were on the way to affected areas as an alternative measure to help restore supply, and that all available resources, assets, and manpower were being mobilized, with priority on accelerating restoration without compromising safety. The Star said Sabah Electricity had not given a number of consumers affected or said when electricity supply would be fully restored, and that restoration works were ongoing, with priority given to works at the site without compromising public safety.
