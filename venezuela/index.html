El Impulso, in a Nov 25, 2026 report, said residents of various Barquisimeto communities went to the Defensoría del Pueblo office in Lara state on Wednesday to protest public-service failures, especially electricity rationing and prolonged interruptions in drinking-water supply. The same report quoted Vidal Querales, a representative of Creemos Alianza Ciudadana and of the Unidos por el Agua y los Derechos Humanos movement, as saying that residents of the El Obelisco, Santa Eduvigis, Santa Inés, and Centro Metropolitano Javier urbanizations were receiving irregular electricity supply because scheduled cuts were concentrated at night, and that, according to him, Corpoelec was applying the load-management plan in those sectors from about 7:30 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. until about 2:00 a.m. El Impulso, in a separate Nov 25 report, said residents of Yaracal, in Cacique Manaure municipality of Falcón state, had blocked both directions of the Morón-Coro national highway for more than 10 hours on Tuesday, Nov 24, to demand that Corpoelec ease rationing schedules, citing daily and prolonged cuts, and that the road was cleared at 6:40 p.m. Tuesday. El Informador, in a report published at 10:51 a.m. Venezuela time Tuesday, Nov 24, and updated at 10:57 a.m. (8:51 a.m. and 8:57 a.m. CT), citing the nongovernmental monitoring group Activos por la Luz, said that on Monday, Nov 23, 26 power cuts in four municipalities of Lara state accumulated 135 hours and 18 minutes without supply across 25 communities, a 54.04 percent increase in cumulative outage time compared with the previous day, with an average interruption of 5 hours and 12 minutes, and that in 10 sectors the outages lasted more than six continuous hours. That report said Iribarren municipality, which includes Barquisimeto, was the most affected, with 16 outages in 16 communities totaling 85 hours and 57 minutes without power, and that Palavecino, Torres, and Simón Planas municipalities were also affected. Portuguesa Reporta, in a Nov 23 report, said residents of eastern and southern Acarigua reported interruptions on Monday, Nov 23, beginning about 7:00 a.m., including an outage from about 10:00 a.m. to about 5:00 p.m. and another after about 5:30 p.m., in neighborhoods including La Goajira and Bellas Artes, and that some of those cuts fell outside the hours residents described as scheduled rationing.
