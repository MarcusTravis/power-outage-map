Kyiv and nationwideUkraineRolling and emergency blackouts after Russian attacks on energy infrastructureUkrenergo said that on Monday, Nov 16, hourly rolling blackouts of 1 to 3 queues, plus industrial capacity limits, are in effect through the end of the day in parts of Ukraine. UNN, citing Ukrenergo, said one queue is four hours, so Monday's outages may last from 4 to 12 hours. Ukraine's Energy Ministry said that after Russian attacks overnight and Monday morning, some consumers were without power as of Monday morning in the Vinnytsia, Dnipropetrovsk, Kirovohrad, Odesa, Ternopil, and Cherkasy regions; it did not give customer counts for those regions. Interfax-Ukraine, citing Ukrenergo, said emergency outage schedules were applied in the morning in the Kharkiv and Poltava regions. Northern Odesa regionOdesa, UkraineRussian attack on DTEK energy facilities52,200 households without power, according to a Nov 16 statement by DTEK Odeski Elektromerezhi. DTEK said a Russian attack on the morning of Nov 16 hit its energy facilities in the north of Odesa region, that 52,200 households were temporarily left without electricity, that specialists were inspecting surviving equipment in order to begin repairs as soon as possible, and that all work is being carried out under safety rules, including during air-raid alerts. DTEK also said the company's production site was damaged in the attack (windows blown out and ceilings and doors damaged) and that employees were not injured. No restoration time was given. This is a separate, later attack in the north of the region from DTEK's Nov 14 statement that a morning attack on one of its facilities there left almost 50,000 customers without power, and from the overnight Nov 15 DTEK attack that left 41,000 households without power in the Odesa region.
