Kyiv and nationwideUkraineRolling and emergency blackouts after Russian attacks on energy infrastructureUkrenergo, in a Tuesday evening announcement reported by Korrespondent at 8:17 p.m. EET Tuesday and by UNIAN at 8:46 p.m. EET Tuesday, said that on Wednesday, Nov 18, planned electricity outages would be in effect around the clock in parts of Ukraine because of the aftermath of attacks on the energy system: capacity-restriction schedules for industry, and hourly outage schedules of 1 to 3 groups for all categories of consumers. UNN, citing Ukrenergo in an 8:07 p.m. EET Monday report, said one group equals four hours without electricity, so those scheduled outages may last from 4 to 12 hours; it said up-to-date schedules should be checked on each region's distribution-system operator. UNIAN also published Wednesday hourly schedules for the Kharkiv, Poltava, and Sumy regions: in Kharkiv region, it said outages would run from midnight to midnight and would last at least 7 hours for some groups and up to 12.5 hours for others; in Poltava region, 9 to 11 hours depending on the group; and in Sumy region, 8 to 16 hours depending on the subgroup. Those Wednesday schedules supersede Tuesday's end-of-day restrictions. No later official Wednesday-morning regional list of emergency cuts or a national customer count was found.
