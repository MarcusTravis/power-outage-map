Eyre Peninsula and West CoastSouth Australia, AustraliaStorms knocked down two major transmission towers and damaged a thirdStorms knocked down two major transmission towers and damaged a third at Wharminda on ElectraNet's 132 kV line between Cultana and Port Lincoln, cutting transmission supply south and west of Whyalla. ElectraNet executive network manager Alex Trento said Friday the towers were built to withstand a one-in-2,000-year weather event and that the localized weather appeared to have been even stronger than the winds they were designed for. In a later Friday-afternoon ABC News report, Trento said the towers that fell were designed to withstand up to around 170 km/h wind speed and were also tested to destruction to their design standard, that he did not know what the wind speed was when the towers toppled but that the probability of it occurring was "very rare," and that a structural fault in the towers was "a possibility" and ElectraNet was investigating.
