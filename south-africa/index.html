This is separate from the earlier Mayfair Substation fire and explosion that began Nov 8; City Power spokesperson Isaac Mangena said Thursday morning that repairs at the Mayfair Substation following last week's fire and explosion have been completedNo official customer count from City Power for the Thursday outages.No official customer count from City Power for the Thursday outages. Jacaranda FM, in a Thursday, Nov 19 report updated at 5:03 p.m., said City Power confirmed it had to isolate the Fordsburg Substation after several substations were affected by an undisclosed emergency at the Orlando Substation. According to the utility, at least six substations in the south of Johannesburg were impacted by the emergency switch-off, including New Eldorado Park, Nancefield, Nirvana, Eikenhof, Pennyville, Industria, and Hursthill. City Power spokesperson Isaac Mangena said technicians were deployed to the affected areas on Thursday morning, that technical teams and operators were on site investigating and assessing the emergency, and that the isolation was necessary to secure the network so assessments and interventions could be undertaken safely. Jacaranda FM said Rand Water, which supplies water to four municipalities, was among those affected, and that it was not yet known when technicians would restore power. EWN, in a Nov 19 report timed at 11:35, likewise said City Power had implemented an emergency isolation procedure at the Fordsburg Substation following an incident at the Orlando Substation, that the switch-off had interrupted supply to substations including Eikenhof, Industria, and Hursthill, that other affected areas included New Eldorado Park, Nancefield, Nirvana, and Pennyville, and that Mangena said an estimated time of restoration could not be provided because technical assessments were still under way. That isolation update is later than a 9:48 a.m. SAST Thursday SABC News report (1:48 a.m. CT), which said City Power reported another major electricity disruption after lines one and two at the Fordsburg Substation tripped, affecting several areas, including Mayfair, Crosby, Brixton, and Langlaagte. In that earlier report, Mangena said technical teams were on site investigating the cause of the fault and working to restore supply, and he clarified that the latest outage was at Fordsburg and not the Mayfair Substation, where repairs following last week's fire and explosion have been completed. Mangena said the latest disruption comes as the city continues to deal with more than 5,000 outage reports linked to storm damage, cable faults, theft, and vandalism; that is a citywide figure, not a customer count for the Fordsburg outage. Mangena's Thursday morning statement that Mayfair Substation repairs have been completed is later than Nov 18 newspaper reports about remaining Mayfair-area customers. Daily Maverick, in a Nov 18 article, and The Citizen, in a 5:40 a.m. SAST Wednesday, Nov 18 report (9:40 p.m. CT Tuesday), both said that while the SABC, Netcare Garden City Hospital, and some industrial areas in Crown Mines had had power restored, more than 20,000 residents of Mayfair, Crown Mines, Homestead, Vrededorp, Pageview, Jan Hofmeyr, and Amalgam would have to wait for at least another week before their power came back on. That more-than-20,000-residents figure was reported by those newspapers and was not attributed to a City Power official count. There is no national Eskom load shedding; this is a localized Johannesburg distribution outage.
