Mayfair, Crosby, Homestead Park, and nearby Johannesburg suburbsJohannesburg, South AfricaFire and a later explosion at the Mayfair substation; after a Thursday-night restoration, the Hursthill substation tripped early Friday because of a network overload. City Power later said the first explosion was caused by cable theft. A separate Crown Substation fault and a Florida Glen cable theft also interrupted supplyNo official customer count. In a Saturday, Nov 14, customer alert, City Power said it had restored electricity to the SABC, Netcare Garden City Hospital, and residential customers in Mayfair West after overnight repairs and testing at the Hursthill and Mayfair substations. A Friday morning attempt to restore more customers was interrupted when the network became overloaded and supply tripped again. City Power said Saturday that engineers would continue preparing for Phase 2, which is expected to restore remaining affected customers if there are no further technical challenges. No official Phase 2 completion notice was found. In a Saturday afternoon SABC report, City Power's Noel Maso said parts of Mayfair, Auckland Park, Crown Mines, and Vrededorp remained without power, that restoration and replacement costs stood at R24.8 million, and that in a worst-case scenario it could take up to 14 days to restore power to all affected areas. Maso also said a fault at Crown Substation was interrupting supply to Mayfair, Crown Mines, Amalgam, and surrounding communities, and that the Sentech Tower in Brixton was affected; he said technicians were working to restore that fault, with normal supply set to be restored by Sunday. City Power's 7:50 a.m. SAST Sunday, Nov 15, Hursthill SDC bulletin said the centre was managing 1,384 open calls, 1,183 of them more than 24 hours old (a wider Hursthill service-area figure, not a customer count for the Mayfair outage alone). The bulletin listed current outages at Roosevelt Substation (parts of Emmarentia along Barry, Hertzog, and Muirfield, as well as Greenside; reported Nov 13); Industria Substation (partial restoration in Longdale, with two load centres still off after a Nov 12 cable fault); a separate Industria transformer issue on Kudu Street (reported Nov 13); and Victory Park (reported Nov 14). It did not mention Mayfair, Crown Substation, or a Phase 2 completion. Separately, City Power said a 33 kV cable was cut and stolen at Florida Glen Substation early Saturday, leaving customers in Florida Glen, Quellerina, Industria North, Bergbron, and Northcliff Ext 19 without power; replacement cable was expected on site Sunday, Nov 15, and no customer count or restoration time was given. No later official Sunday confirmation that the cable had arrived or that supply had returned was found. City Power also said Saturday evening that a cable fault at Florida Substation's Mechanical Workshop Switching Station had left customers in Florida Lake, Hamberg, and Georginia affected, with one load centre remaining off until repairs were done; at 7:15 a.m. SAST Sunday it said operators would be dispatched for a recent unplanned interruption in Florida Lake. No customer count or restoration time was given for that outage.
