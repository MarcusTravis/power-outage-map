City Power later said the first explosion was caused by cable theft. A separate Crown Substation fault and a Florida Glen cable theft also interrupted supply. In a Sunday, Nov 15, Mayfair Substation customer alert posted at 12:15 p.m. SAST, City Power said it would implement a 16-hour emergency shutdown from 12:00 noon Sunday to complete critical repairs at the Mayfair Substation and stabilise the network after the prolonged outage. The shutdown would affect customers restored during Phase 1, including the SABC, Netcare Garden City Hospital, and residential customers in Mayfair West. City Power also said Sentech would remain without electricity until multiple cable faults had been located and repaired. SABC News, in a 1:48 p.m. SAST Sunday report, quoted City Power spokesperson Isaac Mangena giving the same shutdown details. No later official notice confirming the shutdown had ended, or giving a new customer count, was found. At 4:24 p.m. SAST Sunday, City Power said power had been fully restored at Florida Glen Substation, after a 33 kV cable was cut and stolen early Saturday; the outage had affected customers in Florida Glen, Quellerina, Industria North, Bergbron, and Northcliff Ext 19. At 7:09 p.m. SAST Sunday, City Power said that because of unforeseen challenges, the Roosevelt Substation cable-fault outage affecting parts of Emmarentia (along Barry, Hertzog, and Muirfield) and Greenside, reported Nov 13, would be attended to in the morning; earlier Sunday it had said the fault had been located and that jointing and termination would commence, and that power in those areas had been partially restored. In a 3:50 p.m. SAST Sunday Hursthill SDC bulletin, City Power said the centre was managing 1,418 open calls, 1,284 of them more than 24 hours old (a wider Hursthill service-area figure, not a customer count for the Mayfair outage alone). That bulletin also said power had been partially restored in Longdale after a Nov 12 Industria Substation cable fault, with two load centres remaining off until repairs were done and the team to return to locate the fault, and that City Power was sourcing a transformer for an Industria Substation outage affecting customers on Kudu Street, reported Nov 13. At 7:37 p.m. SAST Sunday, City Power said a team was on site investigating an unplanned Alexandra Substation outage affecting customers in Riverpark, East Bank, and Tsutsumani; no customer count or restoration time was given. For Florida Substation, City Power said at 12:10 p.m. SAST Sunday that power had been restored except for 3 load centres affected by a burnt mini-substation and attempted vandalism, with the team sourcing materials for repairs, and that the remaining outage affected customers in Florida Lake; no customer count or restoration time was given for those load centres, and no later official update was found. Earlier Saturday, City Power had restored electricity to the SABC, Netcare Garden City Hospital, and residential customers in Mayfair West after overnight repairs and testing at the Hursthill and Mayfair substations, and said engineers would continue preparing for Phase 2, expected to restore remaining affected customers if there were no further technical challenges. No official Phase 2 completion notice was found. In a Saturday afternoon SABC report, City Power's Noel Maso said parts of Mayfair, Auckland Park, Crown Mines, and Vrededorp remained without power, that restoration and replacement costs stood at R24.8 million, and that in a worst-case scenario it could take up to 14 days to restore power to all affected areas. Maso also said a fault at Crown Substation was interrupting supply to Mayfair, Crown Mines, Amalgam, and surrounding communities, and that the Sentech Tower in Brixton was affected, with normal supply then set to be restored by Sunday; no later official confirmation that the Crown Substation fault had been cleared was found.
