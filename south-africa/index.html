City Power later said the first explosion was caused by cable theft. A separate Crown Substation fault and a Florida Glen cable theft also interrupted supply. On Sunday, City Power said a 16-hour emergency shutdown was needed to complete critical Mayfair Substation repairsNo official customer count. In a Sunday, Nov 15, Mayfair Substation customer alert posted at 12:15 p.m. SAST, City Power said it would implement a 16-hour emergency shutdown from 12:00 noon Sunday to complete critical repairs at the Mayfair Substation and stabilise the network after the prolonged outage. The shutdown would affect customers restored during Phase 1, including the SABC, Netcare Garden City Hospital, and residential customers in Mayfair West. City Power also said Sentech would remain without electricity until multiple cable faults had been located and repaired. No later official notice confirming the shutdown had ended, or giving a new customer count, was found. Earlier Saturday, City Power had restored electricity to the SABC, Netcare Garden City Hospital, and residential customers in Mayfair West after overnight repairs and testing at the Hursthill and Mayfair substations, and said engineers would continue preparing for Phase 2, expected to restore remaining affected customers if there were no further technical challenges. No official Phase 2 completion notice was found. In a Saturday afternoon SABC report, City Power's Noel Maso said parts of Mayfair, Auckland Park, Crown Mines, and Vrededorp remained without power, that restoration and replacement costs stood at R24.8 million, and that in a worst-case scenario it could take up to 14 days to restore power to all affected areas. Maso also said a fault at Crown Substation was interrupting supply to Mayfair, Crown Mines, Amalgam, and surrounding communities, and that the Sentech Tower in Brixton was affected, with normal supply then set to be restored by Sunday; no later official confirmation that the Crown Substation fault had been cleared was found. City Power's 7:50 a.m. SAST Sunday Hursthill SDC bulletin said the centre was managing 1,384 open calls, 1,183 of them more than 24 hours old (a wider Hursthill service-area figure, not a customer count for the Mayfair outage alone). On Sunday afternoon, City Power said power had been restored in Victory Park (Parkhurst Substation; faulty cable reported Nov 14). At Roosevelt Substation, it said power in parts of Emmarentia (along Barry, Hertzog, and Muirfield) and Greenside had been partially restored, then that the fault had been located and jointing and termination would commence. Separately, City Power said a 33 kV cable was cut and stolen at Florida Glen Substation early Saturday, leaving customers in Florida Glen, Quellerina, Industria North, Bergbron, and Northcliff Ext 19 without power; replacement cable was expected on site Sunday, Nov 15, and no customer count or restoration time was given. No later official Sunday confirmation that the cable had arrived or that supply had returned was found.
