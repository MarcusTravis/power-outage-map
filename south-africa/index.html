Daily Maverick, in a Nov 18 article, and The Citizen, in a 5:40 a.m. SAST Wednesday, Nov 18 report, both said that while the SABC, Netcare Garden City Hospital, and some industrial areas in Crown Mines had had power restored, more than 20,000 residents of Mayfair, Crown Mines, Homestead, Vrededorp, Pageview, Jan Hofmeyr, and Amalgam would have to wait for at least another week before their power came back on. That more-than-20,000-residents figure was reported by those newspapers and was not attributed to a City Power official count. The Citizen said the 49 residents of the Jan Hofmeyer Old Age Home in Vrededorp had been without power for nine days since the Mayfair substation fire. Daily Maverick, reporting from the home, likewise said residents there had been without power for nine days and that a water outage was also affecting the area, with Johannesburg Water listing the Brixton tower as empty. Both reports quoted City Power spokesperson Isaac Mangena as saying that full power was restored after midnight on Sunday, that technicians were working on cable faults and on repairs at the Sentech tower, which he said had experienced multiple cable faults, and that engineers were attending to further cable faults affecting customers in Mayfair West, Amalgam, Vrededorp, Homestead Park, Pageview, and Jan Hofmeyr. Mangena said Monday's work would focus on resolving the remaining faults, monitoring the network, and assessing its capacity to safely accommodate additional load, and that once the necessary repairs were completed and the network was sufficiently stable, the remaining customers would be restored in phases. He said City Power hoped to have this completed by the end of the week. The Citizen also said City Power started Monday morning with more than 3,100 open calls across the city, a citywide figure, not a customer count for the Mayfair-area outage. Those Mangena comments and the Monday open-call figure describe Monday's work and were published in The Citizen's Wednesday morning report and in Daily Maverick's Nov 18 article; no later official City Power restoration notice for the remaining Mayfair-area suburbs or for Sentech was found after those reports in a Wednesday-afternoon recheck. Earlier, SABC News, in a 10:01 a.m. SAST Monday, Nov 16 report, quoted Mangena saying that restoration shortly after midnight had brought several critical customers and sections of the network back online, including the SABC and Netcare Garden City Hospital, and that restoration work was continuing. He said parts of Crown remained outstanding and were scheduled to receive attention on Monday, and that technicians were also attending to further cable faults affecting customers in Mayfair West, Amalgam, Vrededorp, Homestead Park, and Pageview, among others. eNCA, in a noon SAST Monday report attributing the update to City Power, said electricity had been restored to the SABC, Netcare Garden City Hospital, and approximately half of Crown's industrial area, that residents and businesses in Mayfair West, Amalgam, Vrededorp, and Pageview remained without electricity because of ongoing cable faults, and that power to Sentech remained switched off for further maintenance and safety checks.
