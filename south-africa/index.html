SABC News, in a 9:48 a.m. SAST Thursday, Nov 19 report (1:48 a.m. CT), said City Power reported another major electricity disruption after lines one and two at the Fordsburg Substation tripped, affecting several areas, including Mayfair, Crosby, Brixton, and Langlaagte. City Power spokesperson Isaac Mangena said technical teams were on site investigating the cause of the fault and working to restore supply. He clarified that the latest outage is at Fordsburg and not the Mayfair Substation, where repairs following last week's fire and explosion have been completed. Mangena said the latest disruption comes as the city continues to deal with more than 5,000 outage reports linked to storm damage, cable faults, theft, and vandalism; that is a citywide figure, not a customer count for the Fordsburg outage. No official restoration time was given for the Fordsburg outage. Mangena's Thursday statement that Mayfair Substation repairs have been completed is later than Nov 18 newspaper reports about remaining Mayfair-area customers. Daily Maverick, in a Nov 18 article, and The Citizen, in a 5:40 a.m. SAST Wednesday, Nov 18 report (9:40 p.m. CT Tuesday), both said that while the SABC, Netcare Garden City Hospital, and some industrial areas in Crown Mines had had power restored, more than 20,000 residents of Mayfair, Crown Mines, Homestead, Vrededorp, Pageview, Jan Hofmeyr, and Amalgam would have to wait for at least another week before their power came back on. That more-than-20,000-residents figure was reported by those newspapers and was not attributed to a City Power official count. Both reports quoted Mangena as saying that full power was restored after midnight on Sunday, that technicians were working on cable faults and on repairs at the Sentech tower, and that engineers were attending to further cable faults affecting customers in Mayfair West, Amalgam, Vrededorp, Homestead Park, Pageview, and Jan Hofmeyr, with remaining customers to be restored in phases once repairs were completed and the network was sufficiently stable, hoped to be completed by the end of the week. Those earlier reports were not accompanied by a later official City Power restoration notice specifically confirming that all remaining Mayfair-area cable-fault customers or Sentech had been restored. EWN, in a 3:43 a.m. SAST Thursday, Nov 19 report (7:43 p.m. CT Wednesday), quoted City Power acting chief executive officer Charles Tlouane, speaking at Wednesday's electricity imbizo in Braamfontein, as saying that outages at the Braamfontein substation and the long interruptions linked to the Mayfair substation have affected households, student accommodation, businesses, and institutions such as hospitals. He did not give a customer count for the Mayfair-area outage. There is no national Eskom load shedding; this is a localized Johannesburg distribution outage.
