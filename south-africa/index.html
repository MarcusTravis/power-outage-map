Mayfair West, Amalgam, Vrededorp, Pageview, and nearby Johannesburg suburbsJohannesburg, South AfricaFire and a later explosion at the Mayfair substation; after a Thursday-night restoration, the Hursthill substation tripped early Friday because of a network overload. City Power later said the first explosion was caused by cable theft. A separate Crown Substation fault and a Florida Glen cable theft also interrupted supply. On Sunday, City Power said a 16-hour emergency shutdown was needed to complete critical Mayfair Substation repairsNo official customer count. SABC News, in a 10:01 a.m. SAST Monday, Nov 16 report, quoted City Power spokesperson Isaac Mangena saying that restoration shortly after midnight had brought several critical customers and sections of the network back online, including the SABC and Netcare Garden City Hospital, and that restoration work was continuing. He said parts of Crown remained outstanding and were scheduled to receive attention on Monday, and that technicians were also attending to further cable faults affecting customers in Mayfair West, Amalgam, Vrededorp, Homestead Park, and Pageview, among others. eNCA, in a noon SAST Monday report attributing the update to City Power, said electricity had been restored to the SABC, Netcare Garden City Hospital, and approximately half of Crown's industrial area, that residents and businesses in Mayfair West, Amalgam, Vrededorp, and Pageview remained without electricity because of ongoing cable faults, and that power to Sentech remained switched off for further maintenance and safety checks. No official restoration time for the remaining areas or for Sentech was given. Earlier Monday, City Power's Hursthill SDC bulletin issued at 7:30 a.m. SAST did not list the Mayfair Substation 16-hour emergency shutdown among current outages. In a Sunday, Nov 15, Mayfair Substation customer alert posted at 12:15 p.m. SAST, City Power said it would implement a 16-hour emergency shutdown from 12:00 noon Sunday to complete critical repairs at the Mayfair Substation; the planned window would have run until 4:00 a.m. SAST Monday. The same Monday 7:30 a.m. Hursthill bulletin said the centre was managing 1,524 open calls, 1,266 of them more than 24 hours old (a wider Hursthill service-area figure, not a customer count for the Mayfair outage alone). It also reported other Hursthill-area outages, including an investigation in Coronationville, Westbury, Newclare, and Paarlshoop (reported Sunday, no customer count); the Roosevelt Substation (Emmarentia Distributor) outage, about 75% restored, with customers particularly along Barry, Hertzog, and Muirfield remaining off until repairs were complete; and Industria Substation outages. In a 7:35 a.m. SAST Monday Roodepoort SDC bulletin, City Power said one load centre at Florida Substation would remain off until repairs were completed for customers in Florida Lake, Hamberg, and Georginia, and that loadshedding remains suspended until further notice.
