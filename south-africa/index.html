In a Sunday, Nov 15, Mayfair Substation customer alert posted at 12:15 p.m. SAST, City Power said it would implement a 16-hour emergency shutdown from 12:00 noon Sunday to complete critical repairs at the Mayfair Substation and stabilise the network after the prolonged outage. The shutdown would affect customers restored during Phase 1, including the SABC, Netcare Garden City Hospital, and residential customers in Mayfair West. City Power also said Sentech would remain without electricity until multiple cable faults had been located and repaired. SABC News, in a 1:48 p.m. SAST Sunday report, quoted City Power spokesperson Isaac Mangena giving the same shutdown details. No later official notice confirming the shutdown had ended, or giving a new customer count, was found. At 9:46 p.m. SAST Sunday, City Power said 40% of supply had been restored to customers in Riverpark, East Bank, and Tsutsumani after an unplanned Alexandra Substation outage, and that the test branch team would be requested the next day to help locate the fault; no customer count or restoration time was given for the remainder. At 9:42 p.m. SAST Sunday, City Power said full supply had been restored at Florida Substation for customers in Florida Glen after an outage it said was caused by a burnt mini-substation and vandalism, reported Nov 14. At 9:39 p.m. SAST Sunday, City Power said a cable fault at Florida Substation's Mechanical Workshop Switching Station was affecting customers in Florida Lake, Hamberg, and Georginia, that the team was sourcing material for repairs, and that one load centre would remain affected; the rest of that sentence was not fully available, and no customer count was given. At 7:09 p.m. SAST Sunday, City Power said that because of unforeseen challenges, the Roosevelt Substation cable-fault outage affecting parts of Emmarentia (along Barry, Hertzog, and Muirfield) and Greenside, reported Nov 13, would be attended to in the morning. In a 3:50 p.m. SAST Sunday Hursthill SDC bulletin, City Power said the centre was managing 1,418 open calls, 1,284 of them more than 24 hours old (a wider Hursthill service-area figure, not a customer count for the Mayfair outage alone). At 12:13 a.m. SAST Monday, City Power said operators had been notified of an outage affecting Paarlshoop and Coronationville and would be dispatched to investigate; no customer count or restoration time was given.
